# Repository-as-State

**Paul Maddison — v1.0.0**

This repository is the deliberately limited public publication surface for the Repository-as-State research paper.

The working research repository, engineering hand-offs, session records, operational notes, implementation details, private experimental material and ongoing post-v1.0 research are not published here.

## Paper

**Repository-as-State** studies whether predecessor reasoning-session continuity is necessary after accepted software-engineering transitions when authoritative project state is preserved externally.

The v1.0.0 research programme is complete. The accepted empirical evidence reported in the paper is:

- P1: 18/30 behaviours in the persistent condition and 18/30 in the fresh condition.
- P2: 0/39 in both conditions, a floor-effect result.
- Subject C: 12/30 versus 15/30 across nine matched task-by-repetition experimental units with three repetitions per task.

These bounded results do **not** establish superiority, inferiority, equivalence/non-inferiority, universal repository sufficiency, resource or cost benefits, security benefits, cross-model generalisation, or population-level generalisation.
